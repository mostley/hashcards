Q: What is Euler's formula?
A: $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$

Q: State the Pythagorean theorem
A: In a right triangle: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

Q: What is the quadratic formula?
A: For $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$

Q: What is the derivative of $\sin(x)$?
A: $\frac{d}{dx}\sin(x) = \cos(x)$

Q: What is the integral of $\frac{1}{x}$?
A: $\int \frac{1}{x} dx = \ln|x| + C$

Q: Express the area of a circle
A: $A = \pi r^2$

Q: What is the binomial theorem?
A: $(x + y)^n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} x^{n-k} y^k$

Q: What is the limit definition of a derivative?
A: $f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$

Q: Express the law of cosines
A: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos(C)$

Q: What is the formula for combinations?
A: $\binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$

Q: Express the sum of an infinite geometric series
A: For $|r| < 1$: $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} ar^n = \frac{a}{1-r}$

Q: What is the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus?
A: If $F'(x) = f(x)$, then $\int_a^b f(x)dx = F(b) - F(a)$

Q: What is the standard normal distribution formula?
A: $$f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}$$

Q: Express the Taylor series for $e^x$
A: $$e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!} = 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^3}{3!} + \cdots$$

Q: What is the formula for the volume of a sphere?
A: $$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$
