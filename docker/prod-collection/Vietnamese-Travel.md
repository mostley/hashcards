# Vietnamese Travel Vocabulary

## Essential Greetings

Q: How do you say "hello" in Vietnamese?
A: xin chào (sin chow)

Q: How do you say "thank you" in Vietnamese?
A: cảm ơn (gam urn)

Q: How do you say "excuse me/sorry" in Vietnamese?
A: xin lỗi (sin loy)

Q: How do you say "yes" in Vietnamese?
A: vâng (vung) / có (goh)

Q: How do you say "no" in Vietnamese?
A: không (khome)

Q: How do you say "please" in Vietnamese?
A: xin (sin) / làm ơn (lam urn)

Q: How do you say "goodbye" in Vietnamese?
A: tạm biệt (tam bee-et)

## Essential Travel Phrases

Q: How do you say "Where is...?" in Vietnamese?
A: ... ở đâu? (... uh dow?)

Q: How do you say "How much?" in Vietnamese?
A: bao nhiêu? (bow nyee-ooh?)

Q: How do you say "I don't understand" in Vietnamese?
A: tôi không hiểu (toy khome hee-ooh)

Q: How do you say "Do you speak English?" in Vietnamese?
A: bạn có nói tiếng Anh không? (ban goh noy tee-eng an khome?)

Q: How do you say "I need help" in Vietnamese?
A: tôi cần giúp đỡ (toy gan yoop duh)
