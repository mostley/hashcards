# Vietnamese Numbers

Q: How do you say "one" in Vietnamese?
A: một (mộht)

Q: How do you say "two" in Vietnamese?
A: hai (hah-ee)

Q: How do you say "three" in Vietnamese?
A: ba (bah)

Q: How do you say "four" in Vietnamese?
A: bốn (bone)

Q: How do you say "five" in Vietnamese?
A: năm (nam)

Q: How do you say "six" in Vietnamese?
A: sáu (sah-oo)

Q: How do you say "seven" in Vietnamese?
A: bảy (bah-ee)

Q: How do you say "eight" in Vietnamese?
A: tám (tam)

Q: How do you say "nine" in Vietnamese?
A: chín (chin)

Q: How do you say "ten" in Vietnamese?
A: mười (moo-uh-ee)

Q: How do you say "eleven" in Vietnamese?
A: mười một (moo-uh-ee mộht)

Q: How do you say "twelve" in Vietnamese?
A: mười hai (moo-uh-ee hah-ee)

Q: How do you say "twenty" in Vietnamese?
A: hai mười (hah-ee moo-uh-ee)

Q: How do you say "one hundred" in Vietnamese?
A: một trăm (mộht tram)

Q: How do you say "one thousand" in Vietnamese?
A: một nghìn (mộht ngin)
